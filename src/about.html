About Me

The Engineer Behind This Site

Background

I'm Nurudeen Adeniyi — a senior software engineer with 12+ years of experience building production systems in C#, ASP.NET Core, and distributed architectures. I've spent most of that time in fintech and nonprofit SaaS, working on event-driven systems with Kafka, CQRS patterns, and EF Core on AWS.

Now I'm deliberately moving deeper into the infrastructure layer — AWS cloud architecture and DevOps. Not because I'm leaving software engineering, but because understanding the systems your code runs on makes you a better engineer at every level.

Why Cloud & DevOps?

What excites me most about software has always been the architecture layer — how systems are designed, how they scale, and how the decisions made at the infrastructure level shape everything built on top. Cloud and DevOps put me directly in that space. I still enjoy writing code, but understanding the platform your code runs on makes you a sharper engineer at every level — and that intersection is where I do my best thinking.

This site is the public record of that transition. Every project I document here is something I actually built, with the real decisions and real tradeoffs explained — not a screenshot of a tutorial result.
